Truth or Dare · Support

How can we help?

Contact Lars Ask at lars.ask@outlook.com for help with the app.

If something goes wrong, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and what you were trying to do. A screenshot can help, but remove player names and other personal information first. Never send your Apple Account password or payment details.

Start a game

Choose a mode, add 2–12 players and choose the group's boundaries, themes and intensity. Couples mode uses exactly two players. Use the suggested names or your own names; nicknames work well. On each turn, choose Truth or Dare, then pass the phone on.

Pause or delete a session

Use the pause button to save and return home. Resume the saved session from the home screen. To remove player names and session history, end the session or open Settings → Privacy → Delete saved session and confirm. This does not remove your purchases.

If the app reports that a session could not be saved, the latest changes may not be available after you close the app.

Change the language

Choose your language in Settings or from the language button during a game. Changing language preserves the current card and player. An optional English line can be shown alongside the selected card language.

Card packs and purchases

The app includes 100 free Starter cards. Optional one-time purchases unlock additional packs. Available products and their prices are shown in Apple's purchase flow; payment is handled by Apple. Purchases require an internet connection. Cards already available to you are included in the app and can be played offline.

Already purchased a pack?

Open the card-pack shop and choose Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase and connect to the internet. Apple may ask you to sign in. Once a purchase is restored, make sure the pack is selected for the game and its cards match the chosen filters. If the shop is temporarily unavailable, try again later.

Boundaries come first

Anyone can pass or swap a card without a penalty. Adult modes and content require an active 18+ confirmation. A purchased adult pack does not override consent, themes or other boundaries. The timer is optional and there is no penalty when it runs out.

No cards match your choices?

The app keeps your selected boundaries. You can review the mode, enabled packs and filters, or explicitly reshuffle cards after a deck is exhausted. Only change a boundary if everyone is comfortable with it.

Privacy questions

Read the privacy policy for local storage, Apple's purchase services, this website and support correspondence. You never need to send your answers to game cards to receive help.
