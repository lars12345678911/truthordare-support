Truth or Dare supports 2–12 players on one device, with a two-player couples mode. Cards and the interface are available in 26 languages. The app includes 100 free Starter cards and optional paid card packs.

You can pass or swap a card without a penalty. Adult content requires an active 18+ confirmation and the relevant settings; buying a pack does not enable adult content by itself.
